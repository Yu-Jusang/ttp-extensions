Under every null, acceptance rates in observed and permuted rows agree to two decimals, as exchangeability requires. Under alternatives they differ, sometimes strongly. Dropping the Bonferroni adjustment keeps the level and changes power by a few points in either direction. It raises power when the extra accepted sources are compatible (Aligned) and lowers it when they are shifted towards the treatment (Shifted, Mean$+$: $0.391$ versus $0.469$).
